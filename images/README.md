# Project image placement

Place finished portfolio images in this `images` folder. The homepage has five matching labelled placements:

| Project | Filename | Image shape |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Threshold | `01-threshold-main.webp` | portrait or near-square |
| Movement | `02-movement-sequence.webp` | wide landscape |
| Topology of Perception | `03-topology-diagram.webp` | square or near-square |
| Two Meters of Motion | `04-two-meters-drawing.webp` | vertical portrait |
| Primitive Habitat | `05-primitive-habitat.webp` | wide landscape |

## Browser-ready formats

- **WebP** — preferred for photographs, renders, and drawings; export quality 80–90.
- **JPEG** — also suitable for photographs and renders.
- **PNG** — for line drawings, diagrams, or transparency; avoid for large photographic renders.
- **SVG** — for vector linework and diagrams only.

Do not place HEIC, TIFF, PSD, AI, or PDF files directly on the webpage. Export them first. Keep images around 2000–2800 px on the longest side and, ideally, below 1.5 MB.
